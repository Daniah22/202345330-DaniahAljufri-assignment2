01 / About me

Designing between story, space, material, and play.

I am an Integrated Design student at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia.

My work moves between digital and physical making. I explore animation, 3D modeling, filmmaking, game design, and crafts. I enjoy transforming ideas into imaginative visual experiences with character and atmosphere.
